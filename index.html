Labour Demand Index (LDI) Methodology

Mathematical synthesis of heterogeneous, asynchronous labour market signals

Synthesizes formal job scrapes, unorganized e-Shram worker pulses, and macroeconomic PLFS surveys into a standardized 12-month forward demand score:

LDI = 0.40·S_portals + 0.25·S_NCS + 0.20·Δ_eShram + 0.15·Ω_PLFS
1. Formal Portals & Industry Hiring Signals (40%) Weight: 0.40

Scraped postings from Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed, mapped to NCO-2015 4-digit codes via NLP.

2. National Career Service (NCS) & Rozgar Melas (25%) Weight: 0.25

Verified MSME and public enterprise vacancies reported to the Ministry of Labour.

3. e-Shram Mobility & Registrations (20%) Weight: 0.20

Inter-district unorganized worker migration pulses and self-declared primary trades.

4. PLFS Trainee Absorption & SSC Reports (15%) Weight: 0.15

Macroeconomic wage trends and official trainee absorption rates published by MoSPI.
